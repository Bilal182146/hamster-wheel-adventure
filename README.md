# 🐹 Hamster Wheel Adventure - SqueekChain Community Edition

An action-packed, fast-paced, mobile-friendly 2D runner game built specifically for the **SqueekChain** community. Guide your adorable hamster through rotating wheels, obstacle courses, tubular tunnels, and cosmic moon bases while stashing juicy $SQUEEK tokens!

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 1. Level Adventure Mode
Embark on a multi-stage campaign across 5 uniquely themed environments:
1. **Level 1: Cozy Cage & Cedar Shavings** (800m goal) - Warm afternoon bedroom setting, soft sawdust puffs, and a gentle introductory pace.
2. **Level 2: Tubular Tunnels & Playroom** (1,200m goal) - Modular plastic playground tunnels, building blocks, and bouncy spring trampolines.
3. **Level 3: Backyard Garden Adventure** (1,600m goal) - Lush clover fields, stepping stones, rolling walnuts, and dandelion breezes.
4. **Level 4: Cyber SqueekChain Mainnet** (2,200m goal) - High-speed neon ledger wheel with digital laser barriers and cryptographic spark nodes.
5. **Level 5: Moonbase Hamster Orbit** (2,800m goal) - Floaty low-gravity physics, starry cosmos, lunar craters, and cosmic orbital jumps.

### 2. Endless Runner Mode
Infinite procedural challenge where speed progressively scales with distance. Rack up astronomical scores, sustain massive combo streaks, and climb to the top of the SqueekChain community leaderboard.

## 🕹️ Controls

Designed with **mobile-first ergonomics** and full keyboard support:

| Action | Mobile / Touch | Keyboard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jump** | Tap screen or press large **JUMP** button | `Spacebar` / `Up Arrow` / `W` |
| **Double Jump** | Tap twice or press **JUMP** while in air | Double-press `Spacebar` / `W` |
| **Roll / Duck** | Swipe down or press **ROLL** button | `Down Arrow` / `S` |
| **Pause** | Tap Pause icon in HUD | `P` key |

### Core Mechanics
- **Double Jump**: Perform an acrobatic 360° spin flip with sparkle particles to reach high bonus coin arcs and clear wide hazards.
- **Roll / Duck**: Hamster curls into a tight furry ball, halving hitbox height to slip safely under low overhead pipes and lasers.
- **Combo Multiplier**: Collect consecutive seeds and coins without getting hit to ramp up your combo multiplier from 1x up to **10x**!
- **Bouncy Trampoline Mushrooms**: Leap onto bouncy spring mushrooms for a super-boost high launch into the stratosphere!

## 🔊 Audio Engine

Self-contained Web Audio API synthesizer generating responsive 8-bit sound effects (jumps, double-jump sparkle sweeps, coin chimes with combo pitch shifting, hit thuds, and fanfare melodies) plus an optional upbeat procedural chiptune background soundtrack. No external MP3 downloads required—works seamlessly offline.
